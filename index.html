Introduction

A life shaped by calling

Prophet Ezechiel Michael Sankinka is a Congolese Christian minister, author, and speaker associated with Power and Life International and the Terre Sainte ministry.

He is known for his biblical teachings, his books on spiritual understanding and the Holy Spirit, and his active involvement in Christian ministry. His work brings together preaching, writing, gospel music, and community engagement.

Organizations and initiatives

The ministry is structured around a founding organization, a network of churches, and several connected branches serving different aspects of the vision.

The overarching ministry covering all of Prophet Sankinka's work — preaching, teaching, writing, music, and community outreach.

Founding Ministry

Power and Life International

The ministry founded in Kinshasa in 2012, forming the base of his ministerial work in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

03

A network of churches founded in November 2019, with congregations in Bukavu, Goma, and Kampala.
